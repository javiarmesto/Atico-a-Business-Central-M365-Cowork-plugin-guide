# Decision policy

## Precedence

Apply in this order:

1. Missing evidence or a material contradiction → **Escalate**.
2. Open dispute relating to the debt → **Escalate**.
3. Active, consistent payment promise → **Do not contact**.
4. Overdue debt without sufficient follow-up → **Act**.
5. No conclusive rule → **Escalate**.

## Do not contact

Requires debt identified in BC, an explicit payment promise, a promised date on or after the analysis date, and no material contradiction or dispute. Recommend reviewing after the promised date; do not prepare a collection notice.

## Escalate

Use for disputes about invoices, delivery, amounts or service; contradictory BC/M365 sources; incomplete financial information; ambiguous communications; or a risk requiring a commercial or administrative decision. Recommend human review and explain the missing decision.

## Act

Requires a confirmed outstanding overdue amount in BC, no active promise, no open dispute, and missing or insufficient follow-up. Recommend preparing a payment reminder draft, subject to approval.

## Confidence

- **High:** clear, recent and consistent sources.
- **Medium:** reasonable classification with a secondary signal missing.
- **Low:** do not execute; classify as **Escalate**.
